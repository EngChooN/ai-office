Features

Familiar, like a real office

One AI session is one employee, a confirm dialog is an approval inbox, a work log is a report, and usage is a payslip.

Claude and GPT staff, togetherBeta

Choose, per employee, whether they work on Claude or GPT. Both kinds work side by side in one office, and the app suggests who fits a task. You can switch an employee’s AI after hiring, too. One account is enough to start. GPT staff are still in beta.

Hire and just ask

Pick a role and a look and they’re ready. Call @name, @team, or @everyone in the group chat — or just ask, and the best-fit person picks it up. Hire one of six ready-made staff from the staff market, or just tell the secretary, “Hire me a GPT employee.”

Approvals for what matters, plus undo

Anything that changes files or runs commands lands in your approval inbox first. You decide how much to delegate, and risky commands are always blocked. If you don’t like the result, one click on “Undo to before the task” restores the files they changed.

Team leads split the work

Give a big job to a team lead: they draft a plan, split it up, get your OK, then run it step by step and report. Follow along on the task board, with the cost shown on each card.

Group chat and meetings

Reports and permission requests flow through chat, and you can approve or reply right there. In the meeting room, each employee speaks with their own AI (Claude or GPT) in turn, and the minutes and to-dos are written up for you.

Computer control kitNew

A staff member with the computer kit can look at your screen and work with the mouse and keyboard. Looking is instant; clicking and typing only happen after you approve in the inbox. There’s also a browser kit for checking websites.

Secretary and tutorialNew

On first launch, a guided tour shows you around and finishes by handing a sample task to an employee, so you see a real result. If it fails, it tells you the cause: login, usage limit, timeout, or network. After that, just ask the secretary — moving people between teams, tidying the task board, redecorating, checking staff instructions — with before and after shown side by side.

Usage, payroll and terminal sessions

See at a glance who’s working and who’s waiting, with per-employee usage and a card for each of your Claude and GPT plans. The top bar shows both usage rates side by side. When usage runs near the limit, scheduled work pauses and you’re told. Claude and GPT sessions you start in a terminal also show up as “working.”

Style

An office that fits your taste

Change the app’s mood with six color themes and light or dark mode.

Six color themes

Pick from Lavender, Sky, Sunset, Moss, Blossom, and Mono in Settings. Only buttons and accent colors change; the layout stays the same.

Light and dark mode

Choose follow-device, light, or dark. When following the device, the app switches the moment your device does, and the window title bar and background match.

A tidier Settings screen

Settings are grouped by category. Pick one on the left and only its items appear on the right. On a phone, categories show as tabs along the top.

Change theme and mode on your PC; the phone follows your PC’s choice. Day and night in the office scene are set separately.

Grow

A company that grows as you work

Staff work, the company levels up, the office expands, and new furniture unlocks.

Lv.1Lv.20

Levels and floors

From a garage up to a five-floor tower. Levels 11–20 unlock 20 new pieces of furniture and decor, with one legendary item per level from level 15. Work mode can hide all of this.

Decorate your way

Buy furniture, wallpaper, partitions, cats and dogs in the shop. Rotate a desk and chair as one set and assign it to a person. Props like the coffee machine and aquarium move and react. Or ask the secretary to “arrange it nicely.” Twelve new pieces arrive too, from low partitions and wood, brick, plant, and glass walls to a book stack and a mini fridge.

Demo Day

On a set day and time, staff step on stage and present what they finished. Results gather in a gallery you can filter by period, person, or lab.

Save your office as a photo or GIF

Save the current office scene as a photo (PNG) or a 10-second animated GIF. You can choose to hide the speech-bubble text, so it’s ready to share. Available in the PC app.

Staff work with each other

A team lead hands work to a teammate, and the teammate walks over to report. Colleagues peek over a shoulder (👀), go help someone who’s stuck (?), and celebrate with high-fives and applause. They sometimes chat about the work, run for coffee, and wave when you click them. It’s only shown on screen, so your work and records aren’t affected.

Phone

Away from your desk, still in your hand

Link the Android app to your PC and use the dashboard, approval inbox, and group chat from your phone — even outside your home network.

No router changes — it connects over a private link between your own devices (Tailscale). You can also limit it to the same Wi-Fi.

You can open the staff market and hire from your phone too, and when a demo day starts, a banner takes you straight to the stage. If your PC app is out of date, the phone shows an update notice.

Known limitations

AI Office is in beta, so a few limits apply. Tell us what bothers you through feedback.

    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    Do I need to sign up or get an API key?

    No. It uses the Claude or GPT (ChatGPT) account already logged in on your computer. Staff work under that account and usage counts against it.

    Do I need both Claude and GPT?

    No. One logged-in account is enough to start; connect the other later in Settings. With both, you choose per employee which one they work on. GPT staff are still in beta.

    Can I use it with no dev background?

    Yes. Hire staff, give work in chat, and approve or reject in the inbox. You never type a command.

    I see a warning when installing.

    The app isn’t code-signed yet. On Mac, choose “Open Anyway” in System Settings › Privacy & Security. On Windows, choose “More info › Run anyway”.

    Won’t staff change my files on their own?

    High-impact actions go to the approval inbox first and only proceed once you approve. You can roll back everything they changed with “Undo to before the task,” and risky commands that can’t be undone are always blocked.

    Where is my data stored?

    Staff, tasks, and chats are stored on your computer. Phone connection is off by default and only connects when you turn it on. The app doesn’t hold your GPT sign-in; the login tool manages it.

    Can staff control my screen freely?

    No. Only staff given the computer kit can see your screen, and clicks and typing happen only after you approve them in the inbox.

    Can I use the phone app alone?

    No. It’s a remote for the AI Office running on your PC, so the PC app must be on. It also works away from home.

    Which languages are supported?

    Korean, English, Japanese, and Chinese. It starts in your computer’s (or phone’s) language, or English if that isn’t supported. You can change it anytime under Language at the top of Settings.

    Can I change the colors or use dark mode?

    Yes. In Settings, pick one of six color themes and light, dark, or follow-device mode. The phone app follows your PC’s choice.

    Where do I report a problem or request something?

    Press “Send feedback” in the app’s left sidebar. It opens a GitHub form with your app version and operating system already filled in.

    Hire your first employee today

    It’s free, and a few minutes after installing you can hand a first task to Claude or GPT.
